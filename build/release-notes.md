**NeuralLink 0.6.9**: new OVERRIDE radar

- **Enemies are squares with their names**, coloured by the game's own nameplate rules:
  - **Red** = hostile or aggressive. These are big, bold squares.
  - **Yellow** = ready to fight.
  - **Grey dots** = civilians.
  - **Cyan** = cameras, turrets and other devices.
- **See what you've already applied**: the hacks on each enemy show as tags (e.g. SHORT CIRCUIT, OVERHEAT), plus a bar while a hack is still uploading.
- **Threat list** next to the radar: red first, then yellow, nearest first, with distance and active hacks.
- **Faster hacking**:
  - **1–8** hit the **targeted** enemy (white outline). That's whoever is under your mouse; otherwise the one picked with **Tab** (Shift+Tab = back); otherwise the **nearest red enemy**.
  - Right-click still opens the full hack menu. Still free during Override.

**Update:** both of you update the app. He closes the game and clicks **Install / Update** on the Host screen (the radar needs the new game-side script).

Bundled third-party mods: [Cyber Engine Tweaks](https://github.com/maximegmd/CyberEngineTweaks) and [redscript](https://github.com/jac3km4/redscript) (MIT).
