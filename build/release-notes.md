**NeuralLink 0.7.3**: mission failsafe, hack vehicles, real enemy names, slower mouse in focus

- **Mission things are protected.** Cars, devices and friendly characters that the current mission needs are marked **⚠** on the radar, the focus tags and in the hack menu. Hacking one by accident could break the mission, so the first try only warns you. Do it again within 6 seconds if you really mean it. Mission enemies and bosses are fought as normal (no warning).


- **Vehicles can be hacked** (Take Control, Force Brakes, Accelerate, Detonate), once he has the Intelligence vehicle-hacking perk. It follows his skill tree, the same as when he hacks a car himself. Cars show up like devices: click one (or right-click it on the radar) for its hack menu. The car he's driving is never listed.

- **Enemies show the names the game's scanner uses**: named characters by name, everyone else by gang and role (e.g. *Tyger Claws Netrunner*, *Maelstrom Heavy*) instead of a generic "NC Resident". This applies to the OVERRIDE radar and the focus name tags.

- **While you hold focus (Space), his camera turns at 25% speed** (mouse and controller). Before, the world slowed down but he could still whip the view around while you were aiming.
- His normal sensitivity comes back the moment focus ends, runs out, or he pauses or opens the menu.
- If the game ever crashes mid-focus, his sensitivity is fixed automatically the next time the game starts.

**Update:** both of you update the app. He closes the game and clicks **Install / Update** on the Host screen (this one is a game-side change).

Bundled third-party mods: [Cyber Engine Tweaks](https://github.com/maximegmd/CyberEngineTweaks) and [redscript](https://github.com/jac3km4/redscript) (MIT).
