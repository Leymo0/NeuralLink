**NeuralLink 0.6.7**: security hardening

- The app's windows can no longer be sent to a web page or open pop-ups. They only ever show NeuralLink's own screens.
- Includes 0.6.6: the room seals itself once Peri jacks in (reopens by itself if she drops), and room codes are 16 characters.

Bundled third-party mods: [Cyber Engine Tweaks](https://github.com/maximegmd/CyberEngineTweaks) and [redscript](https://github.com/jac3km4/redscript) (MIT).
